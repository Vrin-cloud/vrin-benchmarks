# Q6: Based on capex guidance from all five companies, which is making the most aggressive bet on AI infrastructure relative to their current revenue? Cross-reference with their stated AI strategy from earnings calls.

| Company | 2026 Capex Guidance | FY2025 Revenue | **Capex / Current Revenue** | FY2025 Capex | YoY Capex Growth |

### Cross-Referenced with Earnings Call AI Strategy

**1. META -- "Personal Superintelligence for Billions"**

Mark Zuckerberg, Q4 2025 earnings call:
> *"As we plan for the future, we will continue to invest very significantly in infrastructure to train leading models and deliver personal superintelligence to billions of people and businesses."*

CFO Susan Li:
> *"The highest order priority is investing our resources to position ourselves as a leader in AI."*

Critical difference: MSFT, GOOGL, and AMZN sell cloud compute to third parties. META's capex must be monetized *indirectly* through higher ad CPMs and engagement.

**2. MSFT -- "Beginning Phases of AI Diffusion"**

Satya Nadella, Q2 FY2026 earnings call:
> *"This quarter, the Microsoft Cloud surpassed $50 billion in revenue for the first time..."*

Amy Hood on capex:
> *"Capital expenditures were $37.5 billion, and this quarter, roughly two-thirds of our CapEx was on short-lived assets, primarily GPUs and CPUs. Our customer demand continues to exceed our supply."*

MSFT disclosed an **$80B backlog of Azure orders that cannot be fulfilled due to power constraints** -- the strongest demand signal of any company.

**3. GOOGL -- "Supply Constrained, Building for the Future"**

Sundar Pichai, Q4 2025 earnings call:
> *"We've been supply constrained even as we've been ramping up our capacity."*

> *"We have sold more than eight million paid seats of Gemini Enterprise... Our Gemini app now has over 750 million monthly active users."*

**4. AMZN -- "Fastest AWS Growth in 13 Quarters"**

Andy Jassy, Q4 2025 earnings call:
> *"Growth at Amazon Web Services was the fastest we've seen in 13 quarters."*

At $200B, AMZN has the largest absolute capex. Against AWS revenue alone ($128.7B): $200B / $128.7B = **155%** -- far exceeding any competitor's ratio. Custom silicon (Trainium chips) provides cost-structure advantage over NVIDIA-dependent competitors.

**5. AAPL -- "Partnering, Not Building"**

Tim Cook announced *"a collaboration with Google to develop the next generation of Apple foundation models."* AAPL pursues an **asset-light AI strategy**: on-device inference + Private Cloud Compute + partnerships.

### Actionable Investment Insight

**META is the most aggressive by every relative measure** -- without the safety net of a cloud business. Barclays projects a 90% decline in META's free cash flow for 2026.

**The contrarian call: MSFT may be the smartest aggressive spender.** At ~51% capex/revenue, its $80B unfulfilled Azure backlog means the infrastructure is pre-sold -- the only company where capex is *definitively demand-pull* rather than supply-push.

**AAPL represents the hedge.** If the AI infrastructure buildout produces lower-than-expected returns, AAPL's asset-light model captures AI benefits without capex risk.
