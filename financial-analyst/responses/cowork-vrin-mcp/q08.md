### Company-by-Company Analysis

**#1 — META (~20-25%) — Most Aggressive**

CFO Susan Li guided Q1 2026 to $53.5-56.5B (consensus was $51.3B — 7% beat on guidance alone). Q4 2025: $59.9B (+24% YoY, 14% impression growth + 10% price per ad). Simultaneously guided 2026 capex $115-135B and total expenses $162-169B.

**VRIN quote:** Zuckerberg: *"We had strong business performance in 2025"* — described AI enabling *"getting more done than we were able to before."*

**#2 — MSFT (~16-17%)**

Amy Hood: Q3 FY2026 total $80.65-81.75B (+15-17%), IC $34.1-34.4B (+27-29%), Azure 37-38% CC. *"If I had taken the GPUs that just came online in Q1 and Q2 and allocated them all to Azure, the KPI would have been over 40."* RPO surged 110% to $625B.

**VRIN quote:** Nadella: *"Microsoft Cloud surpassed $50 billion in revenue for the first time... beginning phases of AI diffusion."*

**#3 — AAPL (~14-16%)**

Q1 FY2026: $143.8B (+16%, record). Q2 guided 13-16%, GM 48-49%. Cook: iPhone demand *"simply staggering"* (23% YoY). Hedged on pricing and supply constraints on advanced SoC nodes.

**#4 — GOOGL (~14-15%)**

No explicit guide. Q4: +15% total, Cloud +48%, Search +17%. Pichai: *"supply constrained even as we've been ramping."* $175-185B capex is the implicit signal.

**#5 — AMZN (~11-14%) — Most Conservative**

Q1 guided $173.5-178.5B (11-15%, $5B-wide range). Olsavsky caveatted FX, tariffs, geopolitical. AWS backlog $244B (+40% YoY). Beats estimates 15 of last 16 quarters. The $200B capex vs cautious revenue guide = investing aggressively while guiding conservatively.

---

### Investable Takeaway

The ~20-25% META vs ~11-14% AMZN spread reflects management philosophy, not business quality. MSFT provides the most useful DCF inputs (granular segments). GOOGL provides the least (no guide).
