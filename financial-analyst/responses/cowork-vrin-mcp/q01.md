# Q1: Compare revenue growth rates, operating margins, and free cash flow for AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, and META for the most recent fiscal year. Which company showed the strongest improvement year-over-year?

## CoWork + VRIN MCP Response

**Note on fiscal year timing:** AAPL's FY ends in September; MSFT's FY ends in June; GOOGL, AMZN, and META FYs end in December. The figures below reflect each company's most recently completed fiscal year (FY2025).

---

### Revenue & Revenue Growth (YoY)

| Company | FY2025 Revenue | FY2024 Revenue | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------------|---------------|------------|
| **AMZN** | $716.9B | $638.0B | **+12.4%** |
| **GOOGL** | $402.8B | $350.0B | **+15.1%** |
| **AAPL** | $416.2B | $391.0B | **+6.4%** |
| **MSFT** | $281.7B | $245.1B | **+14.9%** |
| **META** | $201.0B | $164.5B | **+22.2%** |

META led revenue growth at 22.2%, driven by a robust advertising recovery and scaling of WhatsApp paid messaging. AAPL lagged at 6.4%, weighed down by declining Greater China iPhone sales, though Services grew 14% YoY.

---

### Operating Income & Operating Margin

| Company | FY2025 Op. Income | FY2024 Op. Income | Op. Income Growth | FY2025 Margin | FY2024 Margin | Margin Change |
|---------|------------------|------------------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| **AAPL** | $133.1B | $123.2B | +8.0% | 32.0% | 31.5% | **+46 bps** |
| **MSFT** | $128.5B | $109.4B | +17.4% | 45.6% | 44.6% | **+97 bps** |
| **GOOGL** | $129.0B | $112.4B | +14.8% | 32.0% | 32.1% | **-10 bps** |
| **AMZN** | $80.0B | $68.6B | +16.6% | 11.2% | 10.8% | **+40 bps** |
| **META** | $83.3B | $69.4B | +20.0% | 41.4% | 42.2% | **-74 bps** |

MSFT commands the highest absolute operating margin (45.6%) and posted the strongest margin expansion (+97 bps) despite heavy AI infrastructure investment, thanks to operating leverage in its cloud business. META has the second-highest margin (41.4%) but saw erosion from surging R&D spend (+31% YoY to $57.4B) tied to AI and Reality Labs losses.

---

### Free Cash Flow

| Company | FY2025 FCF | FY2024 FCF | YoY Change | Key Driver |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|------------|
| **AAPL** | $98.8B | $108.8B | **-9.2%** | Modest capex increase |
| **MSFT** | $71.6B | $74.1B | **-3.3%** | Capex up 45% to $64.6B (AI infra) |
| **GOOGL** | $73.3B | ~$72.8B | **+0.7%** | Capex surged 74% to $91.4B |
| **AMZN** | $7.7B | $38.2B | **-79.8%** | Capex exploded to $131.8B (+59%) |
| **META** | $52.1B | $43.6B | **+19.5%** | Strong op. cash flow growth outpaced capex |

FCF across the group was pressured by an industry-wide capex supercycle for AI infrastructure. META was the only company to deliver meaningful FCF growth (+19.5%), while AMZN's FCF collapsed as it invested $131.8B in infrastructure. GOOGL essentially held flat despite a massive capex ramp.

---

### Verdict: Strongest YoY Improvement

**META showed the strongest overall improvement** across the portfolio in FY2025:

- Highest revenue growth: +22.2% (nearly double the group median)
- Highest operating income growth: +20.0%
- Only meaningful FCF grower: +19.5%, the sole company in the group to expand FCF substantially

The bear case on META is the 74 bps margin contraction and escalating Reality Labs losses (+8% to ~$19.8B). But the sheer top-line acceleration combined with bottom-line FCF generation makes it the clear standout improver.

**Runner-up: MSFT** delivered the strongest *margin expansion* (+97 bps) with 14.9% revenue growth and 17.4% operating income growth — demonstrating superior operating leverage despite a 45% capex increase. MSFT's margin discipline during a heavy investment cycle is a structural advantage.

**Actionable takeaway:** META offers the best *momentum* play, but MSFT is the higher-quality *compounder* given its superior margin profile and cloud durability. AMZN's FCF collapse (-80%) is a near-term red flag, though it reflects investment for future AWS growth rather than operational deterioration.
