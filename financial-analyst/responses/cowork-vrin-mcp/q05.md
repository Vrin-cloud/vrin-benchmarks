### The Answer: Microsoft -- The Only Company With Declining Gross Margins

**MSFT is the only company in the group with a sustained gross margin decline**: 71.4% -> 69.8% -> 68.8%, a cumulative 260 basis point erosion over two years. The 10-K is explicit:

> *"Gross margin percentage decreased slightly driven by continued investments in AI infrastructure"*

> *"Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage decreased to 69% driven by the impact of scaling our AI infrastructure, offset in part by efficiency gains in Azure."*

However, there's a critical nuance: **MSFT's formal R&D line as a percentage of revenue is actually declining** (12.8% -> 12.0% -> 11.5%) because revenue growth (+15% YoY) outpaces R&D spending growth (+10% YoY).

---

### The Deeper Insight: AI Investment Is Migrating from R&D to COGS

**MSFT's gross margin decline is itself a form of R&D investment.** The AI infrastructure depreciation that compresses gross margins flows through **cost of revenue** rather than the R&D expense line.

"Hidden R&D" quantification:

| Component | FY2024 | FY2025 | Change |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| Formal R&D | $29,510M | $32,488M | +$2,978M (+10.1%) |
| Cost of Revenue | $74,114M | $87,831M | +$13,717M (+18.5%) |
| **Combined "Innovation Spend"** | **$103,624M** | **$120,319M** | **+$16,695M (+16.1%)** |
| As % of Revenue | **42.3%** | **42.7%** | **+40 bps** |

When you combine formal R&D with cost of revenue, MSFT's total "innovation spending" as a percentage of revenue is actually **increasing** -- from 42.3% to 42.7%.

---

### Why GOOGL and AMZN Are Next

| Company | Cost of Rev Growth (FY24->25) | Revenue Growth (FY24->25) | Gap |
|---------|----------------------------|-------------------------|-----|
| **MSFT** | +18.5% | +14.9% | **Cost outpacing revenue by 3.6pp** |
| **GOOGL** | +11.1% | +15.1% | Revenue still ahead by 4.0pp |
| **AMZN** | +9.2% | +12.4% | Revenue still ahead by 3.2pp |

For GOOGL specifically, "other cost of revenues" (primarily AI infrastructure depreciation) surged $11.2B in FY2025. If GOOGL's cost of revenue growth accelerates above ~15% top-line growth -- plausible given the $85B->$175B+ capex ramp -- gross margins will begin compressing in FY2026.

---

### What This Tells Us About Competitive Position

**MSFT's margin compression signals the cost of defending its cloud leadership position.** Azure is growing 31% YoY (with 13pp from AI services), and Microsoft is accepting margin erosion to lock in AI infrastructure capacity ahead of demand.

The competitive implication: MSFT's willingness to absorb 260 bps of margin compression means competitors must match this investment to remain relevant. AAPL (at only 3% capex/revenue) is deliberately opting out.

**For investors:** MSFT's declining gross margins should not be read as deteriorating business quality. They're the price of admission to the AI platform war. The question is whether AI-driven revenue acceleration will eventually re-expand margins. The 10-K language -- "offset in part by efficiency gains in Azure" -- suggests MSFT expects to bend the cost curve eventually.
