# Q9: Are there any notable inconsistencies between what management highlighted in their earnings press release (8-K) versus what's disclosed in the 10-K risk factors or MD&A for any of these companies?

## CoWork + VRIN MCP Response

### Inconsistency #1 (Most Material): MSFT — OpenAI as "Strategic Partner" vs. "Competitor"

**8-K/call:** OpenAI celebrated as cornerstone of AI strategy. $625B RPO includes massive OpenAI contract.

**10-K:** Lists OpenAI as a *competitor*. Investment generated $3.1B net loss (Q1 FY2026), then $7.6B gain (Q2). OpenAI has Amazon relationships. 45% of $625B RPO attributable to unprofitable OpenAI.

### Inconsistency #2: GOOGL — Search Confidence vs. Antitrust + Declining Safari Volumes

**8-K/call:** "Nearly every major vertical accelerated in Q4." Search grew 17%.

**10-K:** DOJ ordered end to exclusive search distribution, data-sharing with competitors. €3.0B EC fine. Eddie Cue testified *Safari search volumes declined for first time in 22 years*.

### Inconsistency #3: META — "Strong Performance" vs. $19.2B Reality Labs Losses

**8-K/call:** "Record Revenue," 24% growth. Li promised OI above 2025 levels.

**10-K:** Reality Labs lost $19.2B (cumulative 5-year: ~$73B). Apple privacy changes limit ad targeting. $162-169B expense guide requires ~20%+ revenue growth. Llama 4 underperforming peers.

### Inconsistency #4: AAPL — "Staggering Demand" vs. China Decline + Tariff Risk

**8-K/call:** "Best Quarter Ever," $143.8B, iPhone +23%.

**10-K:** Greater China revenue declined 4%. New tariff risk disclosure section. ~85% of iPhones manufactured in China. Cook disclosed $1.4B tariff cost for March quarter on call — not in press release.

### Inconsistency #5: AMZN — Conservative Revenue vs. Aggressive $200B Capex

**8-K/call:** Q1 guided $173.5-178.5B (11-15%), hedged with macro caveats.

**10-K:** $200B capex committed. Risk factors warn investments may be "written down or written off." This inconsistency *favors* the investor — $200B signals 18-20%+ demand expectations.

### Synthesis

**Genuine disclosure risk:** MSFT (OpenAI as competitor), GOOGL (Safari volume decline), META ($19.2B RL losses)

**Strategic framing asymmetry:** AAPL (China + tariffs), AMZN (conservative guide + aggressive capex)

**Most actionable:** GOOGL — widest gap. Safari volumes declined for first time in 22 years while earnings call projects acceleration.
