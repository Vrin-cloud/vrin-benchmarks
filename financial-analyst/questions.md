# Financial Analyst Head-to-Head: Test Questions

10 questions across 5 categories, designed to test cross-document financial reasoning.

**Companies**: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META
**Documents**: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K (earnings release), Earnings Transcript per company

---

## Category 1: Cross-Company Comparison

**Q1**: Compare revenue growth rates, operating margins, and free cash flow for AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, and META for the most recent fiscal year. Which company showed the strongest improvement year-over-year?

**Q2**: Across all five companies, which is spending the most on AI/cloud infrastructure as a percentage of revenue? How do their capex trajectories compare?

## Category 2: Deep-Dive Analysis

**Q3**: What are the top 3 NEW risk factors in META's latest 10-K that weren't prominent in prior filings? What specific business changes drove these new risks?

**Q4**: Analyze Amazon's cash flow — break down free cash flow generation and explain what's driving the difference between net income and operating cash flow.

## Category 3: Multi-Hop Reasoning

**Q5**: Which of the five companies has both declining gross margins AND increasing R&D spending as a percentage of revenue? What does this suggest about their competitive position?

**Q6**: Based on capex guidance from all five companies, which is making the most aggressive bet on AI infrastructure relative to their current revenue? Cross-reference with their stated AI strategy from earnings calls.

## Category 4: Portfolio & Correlation Analysis

**Q7**: If I held equal-weight positions in all five stocks, what's my effective revenue concentration by business segment? Which pairs have the highest business overlap and would likely correlate most in a downturn?

**Q8**: Rank all five companies by implied revenue growth for the next fiscal year based on management guidance from earnings calls and 8-K filings. Which management team gave the most conservative vs aggressive outlook?

## Category 5: Anomaly & Inconsistency Detection

**Q9**: Are there any notable inconsistencies between what management highlighted in their earnings press release (8-K) versus what's disclosed in the 10-K risk factors or MD&A for any of these companies?

**Q10**: Which company has the largest gap between reported non-GAAP and GAAP earnings? What specific adjustments are they making, and should investors be concerned?

---

## Scoring Rubric (per question, 0-5 each dimension)

| Dimension | 0 | 1-2 | 3-4 | 5 |
|-----------|---|-----|-----|---|
| **Accuracy** | Wrong facts | Some errors | Mostly correct | All facts verified |
| **Depth** | Surface only | Basic analysis | Multi-factor | Nuanced insights |
| **Cross-Doc** | Single source | 2 sources | 3+ sources | Synthesized across all |
| **Actionability** | No action implied | Vague direction | Specific suggestion | Trade-ready insight |
| **Specificity** | No numbers | Some numbers | Key metrics cited | Exact figures + sources |

**Max score**: 25 points/question × 10 questions = 250 points per system
